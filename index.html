About

Translating life
into still images

I am Jaydeeh Maina, a creative photographer based in Nairobi, Kenya. My work is driven by a desire to translate experiences into still images — to capture moments and immortalise the emotions within them.

I love to shoot portraits, nature, events and street photography, always trying my best to capture life itself happening all around me. Every frame is an attempt to freeze something real — something felt.

Open for commissions, event coverage, portrait sessions and collaborations across Nairobi and beyond.
